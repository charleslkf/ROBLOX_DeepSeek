# DNF-DS Game Manual

## 1. Core Concept
DNF-DS (Dynamic Nightmare Fugitive - Data Slasher) is an asymmetric 4 vs. 1 multiplayer horror game. Four resourceful Survivors must work together to escape an enclosed area while being hunted by a relentless Killer.

## 2. Roles & Objectives

### Survivors (4 Players)
- **Primary Objective:** Repair 5 generators scattered across the map. Once all generators are repaired, the exit gates will be powered. Survivors must then open an exit gate and escape to win.
- **Health States:**
    - **Healthy:** Unharmed.
    - **Injured:** Damaged by the Killer. Movement may be impaired.
    - **Downed:** Incapacitated and unable to move. Can be picked up by the Killer or revived by teammates.
- **Core Abilities:**
    - **Interaction:** Interact with generators, exit gates, pallets, windows, and downed teammates.
    - **Skill Checks:** While repairing generators, Survivors must succeed at randomly timed skill checks. Failure results in a loud explosion, notifying the Killer of their location and regressing generator progress.

### The Killer (1 Player)
- **Primary Objective:** Hunt down all four Survivors and sacrifice them on sacrificial hooks before they can escape.
- **Core Abilities:**
    - **Superior Speed:** Moves slightly faster than a running Survivor.
    - **Melee Attack:** A primary attack that can damage Survivors, changing their health state from Healthy to Injured, or Injured to Downed.
    - **Carry & Hook:** Pick up downed Survivors and carry them to a sacrificial hook. Once a Survivor is on a hook, a sacrifice timer begins.
    - **Secondary Ability:** A short-duration speed boost on a cooldown to help close the distance or patrol the map.

## 3. Key Map Elements
- **Generators:** The primary objective for Survivors. Require a 60-second channeled interaction (interrupted by skill checks) to complete.
- **Exit Gates:** Two gates that become powered after 5 generators are repaired. Require a 10-second uninterruptible channel to open.
- **Sacrificial Hooks:** The Killer's objective. Survivors can rescue their teammates from hooks before the sacrifice timer completes.
- **Pallets & Windows:** Environmental obstacles that Survivors can use to slow down or evade the Killer. Pallets are a one-time use stun, while windows can be vaulted.

## 4. Win Conditions
- **Survivors Win:** At least one Survivor successfully escapes through an open exit gate.
- **Killer Wins:** All four Survivors are sacrificed.

## 5. Scoring
Players are awarded points for performing key actions throughout the match, such as repairing generators, stunning the Killer, unhooking teammates, or sacrificing Survivors. An end-game screen will display the winner and the final scores for each player.
