# DNF-DS Game Manual (MVP Version)

## 1. Core Concept
DNF-DS is an asymmetric 1 vs. 4 multiplayer horror game. Four resourceful Survivors must work together to escape an enclosed area while being hunted by a relentless Killer.

## 2. Roles & Objectives

### Survivors (4 Players)
- **Primary Objective:** Repair 5 generators scattered across the map. Once all generators are repaired, the exit gates will be powered. Survivors must then open an exit gate and escape to win.
- **Health States:** Survivors can be Healthy, Injured, or Downed.
- **Core Abilities:** All survivors are functionally identical. They can interact with generators, exit gates, and other survivors. They must complete skill checks to repair generators.

### The Killer (1 Player)
- **Primary Objective:** Hunt down all four Survivors and sacrifice them on sacrificial hooks before they can escape.
- **Core Abilities:** The Killer moves faster than Survivors and can damage them with a primary attack. They can pick up downed Survivors and carry them to a sacrificial hook.

## 3. Key Map Elements
- **Generators:** The primary objective for Survivors. Require a 60-second channeled interaction, featuring skill checks, to complete.
- **Exit Gates:** Two gates that become powered after 5 generators are repaired. Require a 10-second uninterruptible channel to open.
- **Sacrificial Hooks:** The Killer's objective. Survivors can rescue their teammates from hooks.
- **Pallets & Windows:** Environmental obstacles for evasion.

## 4. Win Conditions
- **Survivors Win:** At least one Survivor successfully escapes through an open exit gate.
- **Killer Wins:** All four Survivors are sacrificed.

## 5. Scoring
A basic scoring system will award players points for performing key actions. An end-game screen will display the winner and final scores.
